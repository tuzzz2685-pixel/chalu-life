Smarter, Not Harder — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Smarter, Not Harder ("the game") is a single-player strategy game. This policy explains how the game handles your information.

We don't collect personal information

  • The game requires no account and does not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information.
  • The game contains no ads and includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not track you across apps.
  • The game does not load content from the internet. All code and assets are inside the app.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings (sound and music) and history are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the game removes this data.

Leaderboard

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the choice score of your first Daily Fate run each day (a whole number from 0 to 100) is submitted to the Game Center leaderboard and handled by Apple under its privacy policy. You can sign out in iOS Settings › Game Center; the game works the same without it.

Purchase

The game is a paid download, bought once on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases. Payment is handled by Apple under its privacy policy; we never receive your Apple ID, payment method or any payment information.

Sharing

Only when you tap "Share" does the game create a result image or text and hand it to the system share sheet or the app you choose. We never receive or store it. Share links contain only game parameters (the deck and your choices), no personal data.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
