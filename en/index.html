FAQ

  • Do I need to pay? The game is a paid download, bought once. Everything is included: no in-app purchases, ads or subscriptions. On a new phone, download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account at no extra cost.
  • A friend's challenge link won't open? Install Smarter, Not Harder first, then go back to the link and tap "Open the app".
  • My score isn't on the leaderboard? Sign in under iOS Settings › Game Center. Your first Daily Fate run of the day is submitted automatically.
  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
